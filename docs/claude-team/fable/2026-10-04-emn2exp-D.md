## 0. Verdict

1. **`STEMn2Exp` as pinned (`∀ D > 0`) is not false and does not need a lower bound on `D`.** The
   row-7 obstruction is an artifact of the paper's chain for `S̃₃`, which bounds *all six legs* of the
   6-loop by `(Ĵ + W^{-D}) W^{-d} T̃` and then needs `(1-t) |{c}| W^{-D} ≲ 1`, i.e. `D ≥ (d-2) log_W l_t`.
   That is exactly where the paper's "large constant `D`" (`3_5:437`) is consumed (`3_5:881-886`,
   the term `W^{-2D} Σ_c 1`). The far sum closes **for every `D > 0`** if `S̃₃` is split by the
   value of the profile on the `(b,c')` leg:
   * `A^f = {c' : P_D(|c'-b|) ≤ P_D(|a-b|)}` (this contains the whole floor zone of that leg): the
     contraction inequality exactly as in `S̃₁` (`emn2Exp_part1`, `EMn2Exp1.lean:502-598`) with
     `M = y² P_D(|a-b|)`, `K = 1`, gives `3^d η⁻¹ y⁵ √P(0) P_D(|a-b|)²` — the `(W^{-d}B_{t,0})^{1/2}`
     term of the pin, with room `W^{-d}` to spare; **no sum over `c`, no `Ĵ`, no `D`-condition**;
   * `A^n = {c' : P_D(|c'-b|) > P_D(|a-b|)}`: there `P_D(|c'-b|) = W^{-d} 𝒯_t(|c'-b|) > W^{-d-D}` is a
     genuine tail, so Hölder with `(Ĵ+ε) P_D` on the six legs gives
     `W^{-2d}(Ĵ+ε)³ P_D(r) Σ_{c'} Σ_{c∼c'} 𝒯(|c'-b|)(𝒯(|a-c|) + W^{-D})`, bounded by `(TTT2)` and
     `Σ_c 𝒯 ≤ C₁/(1-t)` by `2·3^d K₁(C_I+C₁) (Ĵ+ε)³ (1-t)⁻¹ P_D(r)²`; **the floor-floor term never
     appears** because the `(b,c')` factor is never a floor on `A^n`.
   Every ingredient is merged (`stContractPt_holds`, `emn2Poly_norm_loop4_alt_le`,
   `emn2Poly_norm_loop3_le`, `ekPropTInf_holds`, `emn2Exp_kellStar_far`) or already in the preflight's
   plan (6-loop Hölder, `Σ_c 𝒯 ≤ C₁/(1-t)`, the shift `K₁`). Scripts: section 2.
2. **Do not restate the pin.** Keep `STEMn2Exp` as is; the repair is in the route of ST2-11 (one more
   region lemma, a copy of `emn2Exp_part1` with the region `A^f`, and the split of `emn2ExpS3`).
   Paper-delta candidate `T2118a` (section 5).
3. If the dispatcher nevertheless restates: the consumers need only "pass a large `D` and weaken at the
   end", because `STprof` is antitone in `D`; but the weakening must happen *after* the whole Grönwall
   engine (`ST_selfImprove_section`) has run at the large `D`, since the pin's RHS `Ĵ_D³ P_D²` is **not**
   monotone in `D` (section 3). Cost: plumbing in 5 theorems + 1 lemma, ~1 day, low risk.
4. **`STLWT` has no such issue**: its RHS `η⁻¹ Bctl^{1/2} P_D` is antitone in `D` and its premises do not
   depend on `D`, so `∀ D > 0` is *equivalent* to the paper's "for any large constant `D`" (section 4).
   The same holds for `STStep2Decay`, `STStep2DecayPT`, `LWtermExp`, `LWtermExpS/N`, `STScaleInv`.

## 1. Why the pin is stronger than the paper only through `Ĵ_D`, and why it is still true

Notation: `P_D(m) = STprof = W^{-d} max(𝒯_t(m ∧ ℓ), W^{-D})` (`Step2Defs.lean:75-78`, `Defs/Tail.lean:47-53`),
`Ĵ_D = STJhatM = max_{σ ∈ {±}², (x,y)} ‖(𝓛-𝒦)²_{σ,(x,y)}‖ / P_D(|x-y|)` (`:81-88`), `r = |a-b| > ℓ†`.

**Monotonicity.** `W^{-D}` is antitone in `D` (`W ≥ 1`), hence `P_D(m)` is antitone in `D`, so a bound
`ξ ≺ c · P_D(r)` at `D₀` implies the same at every `D ≤ D₀`. For `Ĵ_D` the direction is reversed
(`Ĵ_{D₀} ≥ Ĵ_D` for `D₀ ≥ D`), and the product `Ĵ_D³ P_D(r)²` is not monotone: if the maximizing pair
`s` of `Ĵ` sits at the floor (`𝒯(s) ≤ W^{-D₀}`) while `r` does not, `Ĵ_{D₀}³P_{D₀}(r)² = W^{3(D₀-D)}
Ĵ_D³ P_D(r)²` (script 2, last block). So among the Step-2 pins, `STEMn2Exp` is the **only** one whose
`∀ D > 0` is genuinely stronger than "`∀` large `D`"; the preflight is right that this is where the
paper's phrase has content.

**Truth at small `D`.** The pin quantifies over the actual model (`seqHflow`), so a counterexample would
have to be a regime of the model. None is in sight, and there is no need for one: the split of §0.1
proves the statement at every `D > 0` from the pin's own premises. Heuristically the margin is huge:
with `|G_{xy}|² ≈ W^{-d} 𝒯` the 6-loop `𝓛⁶_{(a,b,c',b,a,c)} = W^{-6d} tr(...) = W^{-6d}‖C^*AB‖²_HS` (blocks
`A = G_{ab}, B = G_{bc'}, C = G_{ac}`) is `≈ W^{-3d}` times the Hölder bound `𝓛²_ab 𝓛²_bc' 𝓛²_ac`
(flat-spectrum blocks lose `‖·‖_op ≈ W^{-d/2}‖·‖_HS` on two factors, random phases the rest), so the true
`S̃₃` is `≈ W^{-3d} η⁻¹ (W^{-d}𝒯(r))²`, below `η⁻¹ Bctl^{1/2} P_D(r)²` by `W^{-5d/2}` for every `D`. The
preflight's premise-consistent test (`holder_route.py`) only shows that the *bound* `Ĵ P_D` on the
`(b,c')` leg is lossy at the floor, where the premise `STLWassmExp` at a larger `D'` already says the
2-loop is far below `Ĵ P_D`.

**Where exactly the paper uses "large `D`".** `3_5:881-886`: `Σ_c (𝒯(|a-c|)+W^{-D})(𝒯(|c-b|)+W^{-D})
≲ (1-t)⁻¹(𝒯(|a-b|)+W^{-D})` needs `W^{-2D}|{c : |c-a|,|c-b| ≤ ℓ}| ≲ (1-t)⁻¹ W^{-D}`, i.e.
`(1-t)(2ℓ+1)^d ≲ W^{D}`; with `ℓ ≤ (log W)^{10} ℓ_t` and `ℓ_t = g/√(1-t)` this is `g² ℓ_t^{d-2} ≲ W^{D}`
up to polylog — row 7. Since `ℓ_t ≤ L`, `L^d ≤ N ≤ W^{1/𝔠}` (`Bandwidth`, `Defs/Sizes.lean:168`), the
paper's `D ≥ (d-2)/(d𝔠)` suffices; the pin has no such hypothesis, so this chain cannot prove it.

## 4. `STLWT` (`lem: EWGn2_N`, `3_5:406-415`) and the other `∀ D > 0` pins

Paper: premise `(eq:LW_assm_exp)` "for any large constant `D`", conclusion `(eq:LW_conclusion_exp)`
"for any large constant `D`" (`3_5:407-410`). Lean `STLWT` (`Step2Defs.lean:421-437`): premise
`∀ D > 0, STLWassmExp … D` (equivalent to "`∀` large `D`": larger `D` is the stronger hypothesis, as
`LWPins.lean:272-274` notes), conclusion `∀ D > 0, Prec … η⁻¹ Bctl^{1/2} STprof … D`. The RHS is a
deterministic multiple of `STprof … D`, antitone in `D`; the premise set does not depend on `D`. So
the conclusion at `D₀` implies it at every `D ≤ D₀` (`ST_prec_mono_eventually`): `∀ D > 0` ⟺
`∀ D ≥ D₀`. **No issue**, nothing to restate, and the LW gate (`LWtermExp`, `LWtermExpS/N`,
`LWReduceT`, `Graph/LWPins.lean:290-300, 447-475`) is in the same situation. Likewise `STStep2Decay`,
`STStep2DecayPT` (`+ W^{-D}`), `STSelfImp`, `STScaleInv`, `STScaleAdm` (last clause). The random
control `Ĵ_D` on the RHS of `STEMn2Exp` is the one and only non-monotone occurrence of `D` among the
Step 2 pins (`STNewKLKAt` has `Ĵ_D` on the RHS too, but at the **same** `D` as its profile and with
`∀ D ≥ 0` deterministic — it is proved: `NewKLK.lean`).
