## 2. The new input (R1): twisted loop laws

**Twisted loops.** For n ≥ 1, σ ∈ {±}^n, a = (a_1,…,a_{n−1}):
 L̃^{(n)}_{u,σ,a;(c1,c2)} := Tr( G_u(σ_1)E_{a_1} ⋯ G_u(σ_{n−1})E_{a_{n−1}} G_u(σ_n) P_{c1c2} ) = W^{-d} Σ_o ⟨(c2,o)| G(σ_1)E_{a_1}⋯G(σ_n) |(c1,o)⟩.

**(TL1) Twisted averaged local law.** Uniformly in u ∈ [s,t], σ, c1, c2:
 |Tr(G̊_u(σ)P_{c1c2})| ≺ W^{-d}B_{u,0}.
For c1 = c2 this is (2.87) (p.18) / Lemma 8.4(a) (8.8) (p.72). Note E Tr(G̊P_{c1c2}) is *not* small beyond this (the twisted 1-loop has no ladder; numerically E T̃ ≈ 0 within noise, §3.5), so TL1 is a fluctuation statement.

**(TL2) Twisted 2-loop law.** Define the twisted 2-K-loop

(= Σ_{a'}Θ_u(a,a')·M̃_{(a';c1,c2)} with M̃ := Tr(M(σ1)E_{a'}M(σ2)P_{c1c2}) = W^{-d}M^{(B)}(σ1)_{c2a'}M^{(B)}(σ2)_{a'c1}: the solution of the convolution tree equation (2.49) for n = 2 with one twisted vertex, cf.  |L̃_{u,σ,(a;c1,c2)} − K̃_{u,σ,(a;c1,c2)}| ≺ (W^{-d}B_{u,0})^{3/2}.    (TL2)
The expected sharp form is exponent 2 (as (2.90) with n = 2); 3/2 is what (B.1) needs (§3.3) and is the form to pin. ### 3.2 Folding: twisted loops are bounded by E_a-loops (no new input)

Cauchy–Schwarz over the offset o and the fold vertex, exactly as in (4.3)–(4.4) (p.30): for 1 ≤ k ≤ n−1,
 |L̃^{(n)}_{σ,a;(c1,c2)}| ≤ ( L^{(2k)}_{σ¹,a¹} · L^{(2n−2k)}_{σ²,a²} )^{1/2},  a¹ = (a_1,…,a_{k−1},a_k,a_{k−1},…,a_1,c2),  a² = (a_{n−1},…,a_{k+1},a_k,a_{k+1},…,a_{n−1},c1),
with σ¹, σ² as in (4.3): the folded loops are ordinary (the twist vertex becomes E_{c2} in one and E_{c1} in the other). ### 3.4 Proof route for TL2 and TL1 (what has to be written; every step names the reused lemma)

(i) **Twisted loop hierarchy.** Itô (Lemma 2.13, (2.46)–(2.48), p.14) for L̃^{(n)}: identical derivation (the twist P is a deterministic insertion like E_a). Since the twist is at a_n, in every cut (k,l) of (2.46) the left loop (G_L)_{k,l}∘L̃ is twisted and the right loop is an E-loop (Definition 2.12 (2): the right chain does not contain a_n). (ii) **Linearisation with K̃ := E L̃ (no twisted trees needed).** Let Z^{(n)} := L̃^{(n)} − E L̃^{(n)}. Writing L = K + (L−K) and L̃ = EL̃ + Z in the quadratic terms: dZ = [K^{(2)}∼Z]^{(n)} + Σ_{l≥3}[K^{(l)}∼Z] + {[(L−K)∼EL̃] − E[…]} + {[(L−K)∼Z] − E[…]} + (Ẽ^{G̊} − EẼ^{G̊}) + dẼ^M, where [K^{(2)}∼Z] contains only the n−1 cuts (k,k+1), k ≤ n−1, whose right loop is the ordinary K^{(2)} at vertex a_k; the cut (1,n) has a twisted left 2-loop and contributes the *source* W^dΣ_{a',b}S_{a'b}(EL̃^{(2)}_{(a';c1,c2)})·(L−K)^{(n)}_{(a_1..a_{n−1},b)}. Hence the operator is Θ̃^{(n)} = (3.11) (p.20) with the sum over i restricted to i ≤ n−1, and the evolution kernel is (3.12) with the n-th factor replaced by the identity: one factor fewer, so every kernel estimate of Lemma 4.16 ((4.54)–(4.56)) holds verbatim (they are products over vertices). ### 3.6 Why the cheap routes fail (critical check of design §8, items 1–5)

1. (item 1) Entrywise + Ward: η^{-1}(W^{-d}B)² — confirmed in §3.3 (replace (ii)+(iii) by (F1)–(F2)). The gap is exactly Ψ.
2. (item 2) E_a-loop laws only: confirmed; there is no exact identity expressing Tr(G^*E_aG̊P_{c1c2}), c1 ≠ c2, through E_a-loops: polarisation P_{c1c2} + P_{c2c1} = Q_u − E_{c1} − E_{c2}, u = e_{c1}+e_{c2}, writes it through the projection onto the "diagonal" subspace {(ψ,ψ)} of two blocks, which is UE_{c1}U^* for the 45° block rotation U; but U^*VU has blocks (V_{c1}±V_{c2})/2 in a correlated pattern, not i.i.d. GUE blocks: a different model, not covered by Theorem 8.2. Block translations/phases commute with the law but give the same loop back. M = G − G̊ (or M = (M^*)^*, M^* ≈ G^*) turns G̊MG^* into G̊GG^* − G̊G̊G^*: same-charge adjacency (G̊G), no Ward sum over the free vertex, no gain. Cumulant expansion without multiplying by M (the identity E[(S[G̊]G)_{xy}Φ] = t^{-1}E[(M^{-1}G̊)_{xy}Φ] + Σ_wS_{xw}E[G_{wy}∂_{h_{wx}}Φ], M^{-1} = gΨ − z_t − tm) trades the M-twist for a nearest-neighbour Ψ-twist (same problem) and produces the identity 2𝒳 = t^{-1}E Tr(M^{-1}G̊E_bG^*E_aG) − W^dΣ_cE[L_{(b,c)}L_{(a,c)}] − mW^dΣ_cE L^{(3)}_{(c,b,a)}, an identity between O(η^{-1}W^{-d}B) quantities whose cancellation is the tree structure — useless without the K-approximation of the twisted loop.
3. (item 3) K̃ not small: true (K̃/K ≈ 0.26 at g = 1) and irrelevant: it is deterministic and multiplies E tr ≺ (W^{-d}B)².
4. (item 4/5) Graph route: the scaling order of the stuck graph is 3 (probe 97) and so is the order of the band's I41 graph (n_S = 5, n_W = 2, n_V = 3); the graph bound (B.7) is η^{-1}Ψ^{ord} = η^{-1}Ψ³ for both, while (B.4) gives η^{-1}Ψ⁶: the three missing orders are the averaged light weight (Ψ² for Tr(G̊E) against Ψ for a light-weight edge, Claim 7.9 counts Ψ), the expectation (Lemma 6.1) and (2.90).
