## Answers to REQ-2026-10-05-1536

### 1. Is the gap real?
**Yes, for target 2 as drafted (`LemDecCalEPrec_gijU`: (P-e7/8) uniformly in `u`, with right side exactly `Λ·gexRHS`). No, for the pin.**

1.1 **What is real.**
- `Prec` is `StochDomAt`: the union over the index, including `u ∈ TimeIcc`, is inside `P` (`StochDomAt.lean:61-63`). `GijGEXPTSwap` is `PrecPT`: the union is outside (`Green/Pins.lean:283`).
- Per-time to uniform by a net needs a floor on the right side: `hlow` of `cont_core`.
- `gexRHS(u)` is a random block average of `|G|²`. For `|a−b| > 1` it has no polynomial lower bound: it can be `≪ e^{-√r}` with `r` up to `L`, and `e^{-√L}` is smaller than every `N^{-A}`.
- So the additive grid error `N^τη_G²` in T2193 (a) row (3) cannot be absorbed. That row is correct.
- I know of no merged result that gives a multiplicative comparison of `|G_pq(u)|²/gexRHS(u)` across nearby times; route (b) would need one.

1.2 **Why the pin does not need it.** `E2Hyp` is a hypothesis on **one matrix at one time**. Clause (P-e7/8) is needed only at the matrices where the deterministic lemmas are applied. If those applications happen per time and the *conclusions* are lifted, no uniform (P-e7/8) is ever stated.

1.3 **Why lifting the conclusions works** despite the arbitrary `Jst`. This is the objection in the request.
- Do not put `Jst(u)` in the lifted right side. Use the **realized control** instead:
  `J♯(n,u,ω) := max(1, max_{σ,a} STLK2(n,u,σ,a,ω) / STtailTD(n,u,D,a))`.
- It is random, but continuous in `u` with a floor:
  - `J♯ ≥ 1` and `STtailTD ≥ W^{-D}`;
  - `STLK2` is Hölder-1/2 in `u` with a polynomial constant, from `nl2_loop_sub` and `gdn_K_lip`;
  - `tailTD(u')/tailTD(u) ≤ 1 + |u−u'|/(1−t)` with `1−t ≥ lam² ≥ W^{-d}` (Step-5 regime (iii), `lam²W^d ≥ 1`).
- `cont_core` accepts a random `ζ` (`ζ n p ω`, `ContinuityNet.lean:146`), so `ζ_i := J♯^{m_i}·R_i(u)` is admissible. `R_i` are the deterministic factors of the three conclusions, with `m = 2, 3/2, 3`.
- Here `J♯(u') ≤ (1+δ)J♯(u)`, with `δ ≤ N^{-1}` once the mesh exponent `A` is large compared with `D` and the Lipschitz exponent. So `ζ(u') ≤ (1+δ)^4 ζ(u) ≤ 2ζ(u)`, which is `hclose`.
- `Jst` enters only at the end, through the hypothesis: on its event (union inside `P`), `J♯(u) ≤ N^{τ'}Jst(u)` for every `u` at once.

1.4 **Retraction of 0803 answer 2 (M2).** That answer said: "pass from per time to uniform in `u` by the merged net lift". It was wrong twice:
- the orientation: `STGijGEX` counts both orientations (§63 (2), found while drafting T2193);
- the lift: a clause with a random right side that has no floor cannot be net-lifted (T2193 row (3)).
