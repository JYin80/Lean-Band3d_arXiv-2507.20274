### 2. M1: can the consumer chain meet the floor `(L^dW^{6d})² ≤ W^D` of T2171/T2172, or must a pin change?
**A pin must change: `STLemDecCalEConcl`'s floor premise. Choosing `D` inside S5-09 is not possible, but the consumer may choose `D`.**
- **Why S5-09 cannot choose `D`.**
  - `STLemDecCalEConcl` (`Step5Pins.lean:161-163`) is "for every `D > 0` with eventually `size ≤ W^D`", so its proof must cover `D` near `log_W N`.
  - There the floor term of the crude deterministic bound fails. T2164-audit M1 shows it fails by a factor `W^d` when `1 − u ≍ 1`, already for the weaker floor `L^dW^{2d}`, and D429/D434 are stricter still.
  - Large `D` does not imply small `D` *for this pin*. Its hypothesis `‖(𝓛−𝒦)^{(2)}‖ ≺ J*·T_{u,D}` weakens as `D` decreases, at the same rate as the conclusion. Converting the hypothesis to `D' > D` costs `sup_r T_{u,D}/T_{u,D'} ≈ W^{D'−D}` in `J*`, and `J*` enters the conclusions squared or cubed.
- **Why the consumer can choose `D`.**
  - The only consumer of `STLemDecCalE` is `lem:pf_step5` (S5-10/11). Its pin `STPfConcl` (`:200-205`) concludes `‖(𝓛−𝒦)^{(2)}‖ ≺ T_{u,D}` for every `D > 0`, and `T_{u,D'} ≤ T_{u,D}` for `D' ≥ D` (`W ≥ 1`; the docstring `:195-196` says so).
  - So S5-10/11 can prove the statement at `D' := max(D, D₀)` and descend.
  - The other `D`-dependent inputs of that proof are pinned for every `D`:
    - `STTailtoTail` (`:121-130`);
    - `STDecay` at `s` (`Defs.lean:121-122`);
    - the `W^{-D}` terms of `STDuhamelConcl` (`:295-306`).
- **Change.**
  - In `STLemDecCalEConcl`, replace `(∀ᶠ n, size n ≤ W^D)` by `(∀ᶠ n, (L^d W^{6d})² ≤ W^D)`, the T2171/T2172 floor with `P = L^dW^{6d}` as in D437.
  - It holds for every fixed `D ≥ 2K + 12d`, where `L^d ≤ W^K` is the `Admissible` / `(Main_DEL_COND)` relation of §21.
  - It is a **weakening**: it restricts the range of `D`.
  - Its consumers are only `inst_lemDecCalE` (`:594`) and the registry line (`Test/Axioms.lean:177`). S5-10/11 are unwritten.
  - **Consumer check:** S5-10's preflight writes the descent `D' = max(D, D₀)` and checks that each input above is used at `D'`.
  - Paper-delta: extend D374 / D429 ("the paper's `W^D ≥ N` replaced by `P² ≤ W^D`; `lem:pf_step5` is used at large `D` and descends").
