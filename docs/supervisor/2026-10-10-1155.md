**Why (a) is plausible once written range-uniformly.** The merged BA facts hold uniformly on the whole window `0 < g₀ ≤ Λ`:
- **Kronecker form.** `Ψ = Ψ^{(B)} ⊗ I_{W^d}` (`1_2:614-616`), so `M = M^{(B)} ⊗ I` and `M_xy = 1(same offset) · M^{(B)}_{[x][y]}` (`BAMres_fine_apply` `FlowPins.lean:100`, `BAMfine_eq` `GreenSchur.lean:93`).
- **Decay.** `‖M_xy‖ ≤ c⁻¹ e^{-c|[x]-[y]|}`, with `c = BAct_rate d Λ κ` depending on `d, Λ, κ` only (`BAMfine_decay` `:124`, `BAMB_decay_large` `CombesThomas.lean:509`). This is `(Mbound_AO2)` on the whole window, and it gives the decay `(Mbound_AO)` is used for.
- **Row bounds.** `Σ_b |M^{(B)}_ab| ≤ C`, uniform in `L` (`BAMB_row_l1` `:139`). Ward row identity `Σ_b |M^{(B)}_ab|² = 1` (`BAMB_ward_row` `Ward.lean:108`). `|m| ≤ 1` (`:136`). `κ ≤ Im m` (`BAReal` `MFixedPoint.lean:432`).
- **Stability.** `BAoffDiag_scalar` (`Ward.lean:202`), `baProp5s_holds`.
- **How the decay enters.** In `G − M = −M(√t V + t m)G` (`BAGt_sub_BAMfine` `GreenSchur.lean:219`), after Gaussian integration by parts, the deterministic coupling links `x` only to same-offset sites of other blocks, with weights `M^{(B)}`. These are summable uniformly, and they are where the exponential weights of `(GijGEX_BA)` come from.
- **The minor gain survives inside a block.** There `M` vanishes off the diagonal (`BAMfine_block_zero`), and the fluctuation averaging runs inside blocks (`S^{(B)}(0) = I`). So the band's minor gain is available where it is used. This is consistent with the design's GE3 (iii) reading.
- **None of this uses `g ≤ W^{-ε}`.** The paper's own BA Step 2 uses `M` only through "`(Mbound_AO)` or `(Mbound_AO2)`" (`7_8:2032`, and in the proof of `(eq:MG_conclusion)`). An argument that is correct at `g ≍ 1` with these facts is correct on the whole window. There is nothing to split at `W^{-ε}`.

**The exact remaining gap: a written d = 3 argument for `(GiiGEX_BA)`, the averaged law and `(GijGEX_BA)`, with the non-scalar `M = M^{(B)} ⊗ I`.** It has three parts:
- (i) The stability operator of the coupled diagonal/off-diagonal system (design GE3 (ii)) is identified with a merged BA bound.
- (ii) The fluctuation-averaging and minor calculus are done with in-block minors.
- (iii) The constants depend only on `(d, κ, 𝔡)`, through `BAct_rate` and `κ ≤ Im m`. The design's exponent table shows that `κ` must come from `Im m(𝔡⁻¹)`: 0.262 at `g = 10`, so `κ = 1/2` is impossible there.

### Q3 (F1, pin shape): **re-pin to the paper's shape (primed successors)**

**Why.**
1. **The band shape is stronger than the paper's lemma, and its proof needs a comparison the TeX does not have.**
   - For BA, the Schur expansion re-enters `G_{x'y}` at same-offset neighbours. Solving gives `Σ_{a'} M^{(B)}`-weights times fluctuations at `(a', b)`, each controlled by `𝓛^{(2)}` at `(a', b)`, for every `a'`, not only those within radius 1.
   - Reducing this to the radius-1 window of the **random** `𝓛^{(2)}` (`gexRHS`, `Step1Boot.lean:59-66`) would need a regularity of the random loops across windows. At Step 1 the only loop input is `𝓛 ≺ Φ²` with `Φ` deterministic.
2. **The extra term differs.** The band shape has `W^{-d} 1_{|a-b| ≤ 1}`; the paper has `Ψ_t e^{-c|a-b|}` (`7_8:1942`).
   - For BA at `|a − b| ≥ 2`, the deterministic coupling contributes about (diagonal error) × `|M^{(B)}|`, i.e. the paper's term, with `Ψ_t ≥ W^{-d/2}`.
   - I do not call the band shape false (not checked), but nothing proves it.
3. **The paper's consumers use exactly its form.**
   - BA Step 2 (`7_8:2038-2050`) puts the deterministic `Ψ_t(|a'−b'|)` in for `Φ`.
   - It then passes from `|a'−a| ≤ (log W)^{3/2}` to the window by the regularity `(eq:Psirelations)` (`:2046`) of the **deterministic** control.
   - That is the comparison the band shape would need, done where `(eq:Psi)` supplies it.

So keeping the band shape means proving, on the critical path (G4, G6a), something stronger than the paper with an argument the paper does not contain. D570 recorded the band shape as a formalisation choice at T2256; T2378 shows it is not equivalent. Supervisor 2252 Q2 settled the **event** form (per-sequence `PrecL`), not the right-hand side, so this does not reverse it.
