## L4S3-F1: the S3 roots are `𝒢` with `α` split by an `M` tie; no closure covers them

**Sources (checkable).**

1. **Paper.**
   - `I₄` (`B:30`) is `m W^{-2d} Σ S_{αβ} G_{xα}G_{βy} ∂_{βα}(tr · Ḡ_{xy})`.
   - Its `∂tr` part is `I₄₂ = mW^{-2d} Σ_{x,y} 𝒢_{xy}`, where `𝒢_{xy} = Σ S_{γα}S_{αβ} G_{xα}G_{αγ}G_{γβ}G_{βy}Ḡ_{xy}` (`B:68-70`).
   - The vertex `α` carries both `G_{xα}` and `G_{αγ}`. The case analysis expands GG at `α` in cases (2) and (4) (`B:102-108`).
   - The BA case is called "analogous" with `GGGamma` (`B:118`).

2. **The BA expansion (merged pin).**
   - The third sum of `BAGGGammaR` (`BAGGExp.lean:72-77`) is `Σ_{w,α,β} W_{xw}M_{wα}S_{αβ}(… − G_{βy}Ǧ_{y'w}∂_{βα}f)`.
   - At `(x, y, y') := (α, y, x)` the derivative sits at the `M`-partner `γ` of `w`, not at `w`. So:
     - `T3C-tr_k = −Σ W^{(k)}_{αw}M_{wγ}S_{γδ}G_{δy}Ǧ_{xw}(∂_{δγ}X_{[α]})·tail`,
     - with `∂_{δγ}X_{[α]} = −W^{-d}Σ_{v∈[α]}G_{vδ}G_{γv}` (T2434-prove (i), (iii)).
   - As a graph this is `𝒢` with `α` replaced by two vertices joined by the `M` edge:
     - `w` carries `Ǧ_{xw}` and the tie to `v`;
     - `γ` carries `G_{γv}` and `S_{γδ}`.
   - In the band `M = mI`, so `w = γ` and the `M` edge becomes the scalar `m` of `I₄₂`. That is why the band root has one vertex there.
   - `D1X` and `D2X`, the `∂tr` children of the single-edge `lanlw`, are of the same kind, with `M` edges at `x` or at `y`.
   - **Observation (not checked numerically).** `T3C-tr_0 + D1X` is a single graph. Rename `α → w`, `α' → γ`, `β → δ` in `D1X`; the two terms then sum to `−G_{xw}M_{wγ}S_{γδ}G_{δy}(∂_{δγ}X_{[w]})·tail`, with `G_{xw}` uncircled.
     - This is exactly the band `I₄` with `mδ_{wγ}` replaced by `M_{wγ}`.
     - So the natural BA root is "`𝒢` with `α` split, `G` edges uncircled", and its partition gives both families.
     - The L4d2 1a checks this; if it holds, there are fewer roots.

3. **The root that was built.**
   - `baG5Graph k s` (`BAExpandId.lean:75-83`) is `𝒢` unsplit: `BAGraph (Fin 2) (Fin 3)` with `mdot := []`.

   - §3–§4 then built and closed the unsplit `𝒢`.

4. **The counters at the root are unchanged.**
   - `ord = n_S + 2(n_W − n_A)` (`BAVocab.lean:146`), and `M`-dotted edges join atoms (`:92-108`).
   - `T3C-tr_0`, all edges circled: `(n_S, n_W, n_A) = (5, 2, 3)`, with atoms `{w,γ}`, `{v}`, `{δ}`. So `ord = 3`. This is the `(5,2,3)` parent of T2398 §3.4: margin −2 at `x ≠ y`.

   - So the targets and margins are those of the merged table. The 1a's sentence "the `M` edges change the counters" is true of the vertex data, but not of `ord`.

     - The T2434 1a measured only the root shapes (`s3root.py`).
