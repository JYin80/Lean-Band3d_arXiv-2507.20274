### B1. Are T2208a and T2208b right? Both are right (argued).
**T2208a.**
- The hypotheses of `UNStep1GoodC′` observe three things:
  - `H`, through `UNNormBound sz M.toUNModel`;
  - `m`, through `UNDens′`;
  - `stieltjesN(ouInit)`, through `UNTrLocalInit`.
- They never observe `‖mean‖` by itself.
- **The construction works.**
  - `ouInit′ = ouInit + (1−a)R·E₁₁`.
  - A rank-one change moves `N⁻¹tr G` by at most `π/(Nη)`, and `Bctl n (1−η) ≥ W^{-d}(L^dη)⁻¹ = (Nη)⁻¹`. So `UNTrLocalInit` transfers at `τ/2`.
  - Clause (2.3) then fails surely.
- **The base model exists.** `H = mean = diag γ_n` is a valid `UNModel`: any probability `μ` is allowed, and measurability is trivial. Hence `UNStep1GoodC′` is false.

**T2208b.**
- **Size of the shift.**
  - `msc(i) = i(√5−1)/2` and `msc′ = −msc/(2msc + z)`, so `|msc(i) + i·msc′(i)| = 0.342`.
  - `a⁻¹msc(z/a) − msc(z) = (t*/2)(msc + z·msc′) + O(t*²)`.
- **Tolerance.** At `Im z = 1` it is `W^τ Bctl n 0 ≤ W^τ(L^d+1)/N`.
- **When it fails.** `UNTrLocalInit` fails whenever `N^{τ_s} ≫ W^τL^d`. Every admissible sequence has such a `τ_s < 1`:
  - bounded `L`: every `τ_s`;
  - `sz0`: `τ_s > 1/6`.
- **Consequences.**
  - `UNTrLocalInit` is false for the band model.
  - `inst_coreC_band` and `inst_coreC_band′` are vacuous.
  - For BA the same `O(t*)` shift is plausible.
- **My errors.** My 1651 3.1 listed `UNTrLocalInit` as "unchanged", and my 1651 2.2 C-form bullet relied on it. Both were wrong.
