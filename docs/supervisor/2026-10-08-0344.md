### Q2 (BA-P3 large-`g` symbol gap): the route is right and closes the gap. It is a paper-delta (missing argument, true statement).

- **Gap in the paper.** `A:58-64` applies the local CLT to the walk `p(0,a) = M^{(+,−)}_{0a}` at scale `ilambda² k`, which needs a step covariance `≳ g²` uniformly in `L`. The only lower bound the paper states is the lower half of `(Mbound_AO)` (`7_8:1891`), and only for `g < (2C)⁻¹`. The theorem is claimed for `c ≤ λ`, i.e. `g ≤ Λ` (`1_2:39`). So for `g ∈ [(2C)⁻¹, Λ]` the non-degeneracy has no source. Earliest visible: T2161 portmap `:1062` (BA-D1); made explicit in T2317 (2026-10-08 02:15 UTC). It does not affect anything merged.
- **The drafter's variance bound is correct.** I checked the derivation:
  - With `w = E+m` and `f_i = 1/(v_i − w)`, where `v_i = g·spec(Ψ^{(B)})`, the averaged Ward identity `N⁻¹Σ_i|f_i|² = 1` (`BAK_row_sum`) and `m = N⁻¹Σ f_i` (`BAMB_trace_eq_sum`, `MFixedPoint.lean:655`) give `1 − |m|² = Var(f) = ½N⁻²Σ_{ij}|f_i−f_j|²`.
  - Since `|f_i − f_j| = |v_i−v_j|/(|v_i−w||v_j−w|)` and `|v_i − w| ≤ R = 2dg + |E+m|`, the bound is `≥ R⁻⁴ Var(v) = 2dg²/R⁴`. This uses `tr Ψ = 0` and `tr Ψ² = 2dN` (`PsiB` is the torus adjacency, `BlockAnderson.lean:45`; `L ≥ 3`).
  - It is sharp in order at small `g` (`s/g² ≈ 2d` in the table). With Target 9 (`BAK_adj_sum_ge`) it gives `Σ_{b∼0} K_{0b} ≥ κ² d g²/(2R⁸)`, with `R ≤ 4dΛ + 3`: a constant `c(d,Λ,κ)` uniform in `L`.
- **Symmetry: only coordinate permutations are needed.** The sign flips already follow from the merged `BAK_zero_neg` + `BAK_symm` (`K_{0,−a} = K_{0,a}`). The coordinate permutations σ are automorphisms of the torus adjacency, so `P_σ Ψ P_σᵀ = Ψ` and hence `M_{σa,σb} = M_{ab}` by uniqueness of the resolvent. That gives `K_{0,±e_i} = (2d)⁻¹ Σ_{b∼0} K_{0b}`.
- **Gap.** Because every `K ≥ 0`, `1 − K̂(θ) ≥ Σ_i 2K_{0,e_i}(1 − cos θ_i) ≥ c g² Σ_i (1 − cos θ_i) ≥ c' g²|θ|²` on the whole torus. The other side, `1 + K̂ ≥ 2κ²`, comes from laziness (`BAK_diag_bounds`). The second-moment upper bound `≲ g²` follows from the merged tails. So P4's local CLT inputs are complete.
- **Placement.** BA-P3 absorbs both pieces, the variance lemma (~150–250 lines) and permutation invariance (~100–200 lines). With a P3 central of 900 that lands near 1300–1400. The ticket should preset a cut at a section boundary (P3a = variance + permutation + per-direction bound; P3b = symbol and gap), counted only if it is triggered.
- **Paper-delta.** Record it (the dispatcher assigns the number) as: "BA walk non-degeneracy for `g ≍ 1` not proved in the paper; filled by `1 − |m|² ≥ 2dg²/R⁴` and coordinate-permutation invariance". BA-P3 may be released.
