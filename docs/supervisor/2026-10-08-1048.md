## Answers

### Q1 (P4 Fourier route on the torus; what `A:58-67` imposes) Sound in substance, but the P4 statement as proposed is the wrong shape for P5, and the tilt must be done on a lift. Three corrections.

**C1 (statement shape; the main one).** The proposed P4 bound `K^n(0,a) ≤ C((g²n)^{-d/2}∧1) exp(−c(|a|²/(g²n)∧|a|)) + L^{-d}` for all `n` is **too weak for `BAProp5`**. Summing the flat floor gives `Σ_n t^n L^{-d} = L^{-d}/(1−t)` with no factor `e^{−c|a|/ℓ_t}`. When `ℓ_t = g/√(1−t) < L` and `|a| ≍ L`, the target `C B_{t,|a|} e^{−c|a|/ℓ_t}` is smaller than `L^{-d}/(1−t)` by about `x^{-2} e^{c x}`, `x = L√(1−t)/g → ∞`. The merged band chain avoids this with **two regimes**, and P4 must deliver the same two:
- (i) `τ ≤ L²`: `≤ C min(1, τ^{-d/2}) e^{−c min(|a|²/τ, |a|)}`, **no floor** (`kProd_le`, `HeatProduct.lean:439`);
- (ii) `τ ≥ L²`: `|· − L^{-d}| ≤ C L^{-d} e^{−cτ/L²}`, plus the unit-difference versions (`kProd_gap`, `:626`);

  where `τ` is the diffusive time (`g²n`, or `γs`, see C2). Then `LGTail` (`LaplaceGauss.lean:71`) pays for the zero mode exactly as in `Prop5Hold`. The cut is `g²n ≥ L²` (`τ ≥ L²`), **not `n ≥ L²`** as written in the request's P5 row. `g ∈ (0, Λ]` reaches small `g`, where the two differ by `g^{-2}`.

**C2 (continuous time; avoids a wrong path in P5).** `LaplaceGauss` is Θ-free, but all its lemmas are **τ-integrals** (`∫ e^{−ετ} … dτ`), and `Prop5Hold`/`PropUnit` consume `Theta_eq_laplace_prod` (`Θ = ∫ e^{−(1−t)s} K_{γs} ds`). With the discrete series `Θ = Σ t^n K^n` they do not instantiate: P5 would need sum–integral comparisons for non-monotone summands, and the discrete on-diagonal bound needs laziness (`K̂ ≥ −1+2κ²`) to control the `θ ≈ π` end. **Poissonize instead**: let `P_s := e^{−s} Σ_n sⁿ/n! Kⁿ` (entrywise series of finite matrices). Then:
- `∫₀^∞ e^{−(1−t)u} P_{tu} du = Σ_n tⁿ Kⁿ = Θ^{(+,−)}_t`, by Tonelli and the Gamma integral per `n`. This is the exact twin of `Theta_eq_laplace_prod`, with `τ = γ s`, `γ := t g²` (any `γ ≍ t g²` works).
- The symbol of `P_s` is `e^{−s(1−K̂)} ≤ e^{−c g² s |θ|²}` from `BAK_gap` alone, so laziness is not needed.
- `LGConvA/B/C` are stated with the band's `lgGam`. P5 restates them for `γ = t g²` (a few lines; `LGConvA` holds with `C = C(Λ)` since `t > (1+Λ²)^{-1}` in the regime `ε < 1`).

**C3 (the tilt lives on a lift, not on the torus).** `e^{λ·a}` is not defined on `Z_L^d`, and the dual torus is discrete, so "analytic in a strip `|Im θ| ≤ c`" has no meaning there. `A:58-67` runs the walk on `Z^d` with `p(0,a) = M^{(+,−)}_{0a}` and projects. But `K = |M_L|²` depends on `L` (through `M_L` and through `m`, `BASelf d L`) and is **not** the projection of a fixed `Z^d` kernel. That step of the paper needs a paper-delta, and the following repair works:
- Define the **centered lift** `K̃(0,y) := K(0, y mod L)` for `y` in the centered box, with ties at `±L/2` split evenly so that `K̃` is symmetric (`BAK_zero_neg`). Then `|y|₁ = zdistD(y mod L)`, the torus walk is the projection of the `Z^d` walk with i.i.d. increments, and `|X|_torus ≤ |Σ ỹ_i|₁`.
- Chernoff per coordinate: `Σ_a K_{0a}(cosh(λ ã_j) − 1) ≤ C g² λ²` for `|λ| ≤ c₀ := BAct_rate/2`. This follows from `BAK_exp_moment_le` (the diagonal term cancels). It gives `P_s(0, {|a_j|_L ≥ r}) ≤ 2 exp(−λr + C g² λ² s)`, hence the tail `≤ 2d exp(−c min(r²/(g²s), r))`.
- All of this is finite sums over increment sequences; no Fourier integral on `T^d` is needed.
- On-diagonal (torus Fourier, finite sums, factorising over coordinates, so `HeatTorus1D`-type 1D sums are reusable): `sup_a P_s(0,a) ≤ L^{-d} Σ_k e^{−c g² s|θ_k|²} ≤ C min(1, (g²s)^{-d/2}) + L^{-d}`. The floor is harmless in regime (i).
- Combination: by the semigroup property and `|a| ≤ |b| + |a−b|`, `P_s(0,a) ≤ 2 sup P_{s/2} · tail_{s/2}(|a|/2)`. This is the argument "below (8.3) of [DYYY25]" cited at `A:64`.

The local CLT of `A:62` is not needed (its constants are not uniform over the family `(L, g, E, m)` anyway). The `ilambda² = g²` scaling is consistent: `BAK_dir_ge` gives `K_{0,±e_i} ≥ c g²` and `BAK_second_moment` gives `Σ K|a|² ≤ C g²`, both uniform in `L` and `g ∈ (0, Λ]`. Record the lift as a paper-delta in P4's report (`A:58-67`: "walk on `Z^d` with `p = M^{(+,−)}`" is read as the centered lift of the torus kernel).

### Q2 (P5/P7 zero mode) Yes, the same as for `S^{(B)}`, under C1/C2.

`K` is symmetric, doubly stochastic and translation invariant, so the zero mode is the constant vector. The gap `1 − K̂(θ_k) ≥ c g² |θ_k|² ≥ c g² (2π/L)²` for `k ≠ 0` gives regime (ii). `Σ_b Θ(0,b) = (1−t)^{-1}`, so `BATheta0 = Θ − L^{-d}(1−t)^{-1}` (for `σ₁ ≠ σ₂`) and `Θ̊ = ∫ e^{−(1−t)u}(P_{tu} − L^{-d}) du`. Regime (i) is bounded by `P + L^{-d}` (the piece `L^{-d}·L²` is at most `C(|a|+1)^{-(d−2)}`); regime (ii) is the gap plus `LGTail`. That is `Prop5Hold`'s zero-mode part line for line. In continuous time, the laziness `K_00 ≥ κ²` plays no role; non-product only matters for differences (Q3). **P7 should be merged into P5**, as the band did (PT-F1 = T2023 proved properties 5 and 8 together).

### Q3 (P6 regularity) Yes: P6 needs an input that nobody in the current design produces. Pin it now.

`PropUnit` integrates `kProd_diff1_le`/`kProd_diff2_le` (unit differences of the heat kernel, with Gaussian–exponential decay). Those come from the **product structure**, which `K` does not have. The proposed P4 gives only bounds for the nonnegative kernel, and C3's Chernoff argument does not transfer to signed differences. The `(|b|, |a−b|)` split loses the factor `(g²s)^{-1/2}` in the region where the gradient sits on the far factor. Two things make this cheaper than a full twin:
- Properties 6/7 need only **polynomial** decay. A unit-difference bound `|∇_j P_s(0,a)| ≤ C min(1, τ^{-(d+1)/2}) (1 + |a|²/τ)^{-M}` (and `τ^{-(d+2)/2}` for second differences), for `τ ≤ L²`, with `M = ⌊d/2⌋ + 1`, integrates to `(g²+|1−t|)^{-1} (|a|+1)^{-(d−1)}` (resp. `^{-d}`). The integral converges at `τ → 0` iff `M > (d−1)/2` (resp. `> d/2`). This needs one new Laplace lemma (a polynomial-decay variant of `lg_bulk`; the band's exponential factor was discarded by `PropUnit` anyway).
- That bound follows by **summation by parts in one coordinate direction** (the `j` with `|a_j| ≥ |a|/d`), of order `2M ≤ d+2`, on the torus Fourier sum of `(e^{iθ_j} − 1) e^{−s(1−K̂(θ))}`. This is the paper's route `A:50-56`, applied to the heat kernel instead of to `Θ̂`. Applying it to `Θ̂ = (1 − tK̂)^{-1}` directly needs a dyadic treatment of the `(ε + |θ|²)^{-1}` singularity; do not take that path.
- The **regularity needed**: one-directional discrete `θ_j`-differences of `K̂`, `|Δ_j^k K̂(θ)| ≤ C_k g² · (|θ| for k = 1; 1 for 2 ≤ k ≤ d+2)`, uniform in `L` and `g ∈ (0, Λ]`. The second moment alone does not give this for general `d`. It follows for all orders from `BAK_off_le` (off-diagonal entries `≤ A g² e^{−2c|a|}`), so no new input from P2/P3 is required.
- The regime-(ii) differences (`L^{-(d+1)}`, `L^{-(d+2)}` times `e^{−cτ/L²}`) are plain torus sums and belong with C1 (ii).
