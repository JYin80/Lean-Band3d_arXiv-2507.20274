### Q1 (is T2325's counting sound?) Yes in method and order of magnitude; my 0344 projection was the wrong one. Three corrections, two upward.

**Why 0344 was low.** It scaled the T2161 row centrals by class ratios and did not look at the source those rows twin. `Induction/` doubled after T2161 (57k → 114k decl-lines; 130k file lines today), so any estimate anchored on T2161 rows inherits the 2× error. **What holds up.**
- The independent ticket-count cross-check (T2325 §7 (1)) is the strongest evidence and does not depend on any line ratio: 95 merges for ST Steps 2–6, and the BA S-chain took 6–9 tickets for ST-1's 9, so the BA chain is 64–95 tickets. Route I chain 78, route T chain 92 sit inside that band.
- Tickets = lines/1000 is consistent with what merged: 891 lines per BA code ticket on average; the S-chain ports ran ≈ 960 (8618 lines / 9). Ports are not faster per ticket than new code here.
- "Every ST file in the endpoint cones needs a BA analogue": sound for the cones (59% of `Induction/`); the remaining 41% are proofs of the pins the endpoints take as hypotheses, which BA also has to discharge. I checked for dead weight: only 2 files carry superseded/deprecated markers, and every import leaf of `Induction/` is a pin-producing endpoint. The source is not inflated by abandoned routes.
- Graph twin (31k lines): the scalar-`M` hypothesis of `LWGtoAG`/`lwClaimSize` fails at BA data (compiled, `scalarM_hyp_fails_at_baD`). This is a real model difference, not just bookkeeping (BA's `M` has nonzero neighbour entries, which is also what made BA-P3's walk non-degenerate).

**Corrections.**
1. **(Upward, the main one.) The method is inconsistent across groups.** The chain and graph rows are sized from the source (0.8 × ST/LW lines). The K and G rows of group A are sized from the T2161 centrals × 1.0. T2325 §8 itself puts the K/G source-based twin at 37.7k lines against 12.5k central, i.e. **+20 to +25 tickets**. Applied consistently, route T is about **195–200** and route I about **185**. So 175 is not a conservative cap. It holds only if the K/G rows come in the way the merged deterministic rows did (0.43–0.88). Nothing merged in K or G2–G6 supports that yet (G1 is the probability-free part).
2. **(Upward, small.) Route I counts the 7 publishing tickets, but they are ST/LW-gate tickets that edit merged files.** They should be counted in the gate they edit (ST, LW) and in the BA total for the decision, not dropped from either. No change to 163, but ST/LW each get +2 to +5 rows that are not in their plans.
3. **(Downward, unmeasured.) A 0.8 twin ratio on Steps 3–4 treats ST's proof architecture as necessary.** ST-3 ran 45+ tickets, with an event-form re-pin and four regimes (§132–§133). The 23 Q-files (32.9k lines, one regime family) are the largest single block, and 62% of the Step 3–4 cone is private. A BA port of a finished proof could restructure it. It is exactly what the G-in-place pilot should measure — **but not on `ST_selfImprove_section` alone** (Step 2, 399 lines, 4% private). See O2.

Net: central ≈ 175, honest range ≈ 140–225, with the K/G upside pushing the central toward ≈ 195 if those rows behave like the chain.
