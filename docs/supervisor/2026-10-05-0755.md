**Gap 1 (`Φ²`; this confirms the request).**
- The controls `XL`, `XLK` are only upper bounds. A valid route must therefore give a bound of degree at most 1 in `XLK` when `XL` is fixed.
- The merged route cannot. Clause (G2) of `GoodSetN` forces `ΓΦ ≥ Ξ̂^{(𝓛−𝒦)}_m` for `m < k`. Membership with high probability (`GridGoodNConcl`, hypothesis `hY`) forces `Φ ≥` every control, the current length included.
- `dDriftNonAltN` then carries `kΓ³Φ²`, and `budgetNonAltN` carries `N^{ε₀}Φ²` (D449).
- **Instance.** Let the true loops be `O(1)`, `XL ≡ 1`, and `XLK ≡ N^δ`, which is a valid control. The pin asks for `≺ N^δ`, which is true. The route gives at least `N^{2δ−2ε₁}`, so it fails for `δ > 2ε₁ + ε`.
- **The same single `Φ` also feeds (D1) and (D3).** Because `hY` includes `m = k`, the *linear* part of the budget also carries the current-length control, with coefficient `N^{ε₀}` instead of `B^{1/6}`. A linear (D2′) alone does not cure this: (D1) and (D3) have to be re-levelled as well.

**Gap 2 (the random multi-time term; new, independent of (D2)).**
- **The event to bound.**
  - Let `X(u) = max_{σ nal, a} |(𝓛−𝒦)^{(n)}(√u X)|/B_u^n` and `S(u) = sup_{w∈[s,u]} Ξ̂_n(√w X)`.
  - The failure event `{X(u) > N^ε(B_u^{1/6} S(u) + R(u))}` is an intersection over `w` of two-time events of the coupling.
- **Why the walk does not help.**
  - The walk supports the stopped-martingale argument, but its joint law across times is not the coupling's. Only single-time events, and unions of them, transfer.
  - The walk's drift contains `B_{u_j}^{1/6} Ξ̂_n(H_j)` at *earlier* grid times, and nothing bounds these by `Ξ̂_n` at the end time. So even the stronger single-time form `X(u) ≺ B_u^{1/6} Ξ̂_n(u) + R`, which would imply the pin because `w = u` lies in `[s,u]`, is out of reach.
- **Why deterministic levels do not help.**
  - Knowing "`Ξ̂_n ≺ θ ⇒ X ≺ B^{1/6}θ + R`" for every deterministic `θ` does not give "`X ≺ B^{1/6}S + R`".
  - Two-point example: `P(A) = 1/2`, `ξ = N·1_A`, `ζ = 1 + N·1_{Aᶜ}`. Every deterministic `y` with `ζ ≺ y` has `y ≥ N^{1−ε}`, so `ξ ≺ y`; yet `ξ ≺ ζ` fails on `A`.
  - Slicing `S ∈ [θ, 2θ)` needs `P(X > …, S < 2θ)`, which is again a joint event of the coupling. The walk only gives the corresponding joint event for the walk itself.
- **Consequence.** No route that §7 allows reaches `STNQConcl` as pinned, with or without a linear (D2′). Repair A keeps `STNQConcl` as the target of S3-12b, so S3-12b cannot be completed.
- **`STXiBoot` is not affected.** Its right side is deterministic (`Step34Pins.lean:414-423`). Its proof has to do the absorption with deterministic levels (answer 3).

## Answers to REQ-2026-10-05-0719

### 1. Is the obstruction real?
**Yes (Gap 1).** `STNQConcl` cannot be reached from the merged `GoodSetN`/`budgetNonAltN`.
- With one level `Φ`, the drift bound is quadratic in the controls. Its linear part also carries the current length with coefficient `N^{ε₀}`.
- The pin's right side is degree 1 in `XLK` for fixed `XL`. Its only self-term is `B^{1/6}·(current length)`.
- **There is a second, independent obstruction (Gap 2):** the random multi-time self-term of the pin.

### 2. Does repair A reach `STNQConcl` as pinned?
**No (Gap 2).** Its (D2′) part is sound, however, and is reusable once the pin is changed.
- **(D2′) holds with high probability at each grid time.** Its level is `Φ₂ = Σ_{n'} XLK(k+2−n')(XL(n₁')XL(n₂'))^{1/2} + B_u^{1/6} Φ_cur`. It follows from:
  - the merged `stSEforLn_holds`, conjunct 3 of `STSEforLnConcl` (`Step34Pins.lean:374-380`, whose right side is random);
  - the hypotheses `Ξ̂ ≺ XL, XLK` (`StochDomAt.mul`);
  - `map_pathH_eq` and a union over at most `N^C` grid times.
  This is the same derivation `gridGoodN_holds` uses for (D2).
  **Requirement:** `Φ_cur` must be a *deterministic* control of the current length `Ξ̂^{(𝓛−𝒦)}_k`, and the current pin has none.
- **It propagates.**
  - The exit time of `GoodSetN ∩ {(D2′)}` is the merged generic `gridExitTauN` (any family `G`).
  - The sub-Gaussian proxy comes from the generic `azumaProxy_subG_ugen` (`AzumaProxyN.lean:980`). `azumaProxy_subG_goodExit` and `subGaussStop_nonAltN` are one-line wrappers for `goodExitTauN` and need primed copies, plus measurability of the new clause (copy the private `gridGood_meas_*` helpers).
  - The QV majorant uses only (D4)/`Λ` and (Vb), so it is unchanged.
  - `GridAssemblyHypN`/`AssembledN` accept any `τ` and a pathwise `dDrift`.
  - `tbDriftN` accepts any level `dd j ≤ a B_j^k/η + b`.
  So the budget becomes linear by instantiating merged generic lemmas, which is less than the ~900 lines estimated for S3-11b.
- **The "`k = 2` problem".**
  - For `n ∈ {2,3}`, the `(𝓛−𝒦)×(𝓛−𝒦)` term is only `(𝓛−𝒦)^{(n)} · W^dΣ|(𝓛−𝒦)^{(2)}| ≺ B^{1/6} Ξ̂_n/η` (`3_5:1064-1069`), i.e. the current length.
  - RBM2D's (G3) contained it (`m = k`), and RBM2D's pin `STOeqPT` assumed the matching deterministic product hypothesis.
  - So this is not a defect of (G3). It is the missing current-length hypothesis of `STNQConcl`. With a deterministic `Φ_cur`, (D2′) has no `k = 2` problem.
- **One more condition on A.** The level `Φ` of `GoodSetN′` must **not** be required to dominate the current length.
  - (D1) involves lengths `≤ k−1`, and (D3) involves `𝓛`-lengths `n−1…n+1`, so both take levels from the lower controls only.
  - The current length enters only through `B^{1/6}Φ_cur` in (D2′).
  - Otherwise the "linear" budget carries `N^{ε₀}Φ_cur`.

### 3. Is there a cheaper repair?
- **"`Φ ≤ N^{-ε}` on the event": impossible for general controls.**
  - `Ξ̂ ≥ 1` by definition (`STXiLKM = 1 + …`), so (G2) forces `ΓΦ ≥ 1`.
  - Membership with high probability forces `ΓΦ ≳ max XLK`.
  - Only the trivial case `XLK ≡ 1` would pass.
- **A different slicing: no.** Any slicing of the current length is a joint event of the coupling (Gap 2).
- **What works (recommended), in three parts.**
  - **(P) Pin.**
    - Replace `STNQConcl` by a primed pin in RBM2D's form:
      - hypotheses `Ξ̂^{(𝓛−𝒦)}_m ≺ XLK m` for `1 ≤ m ≤ n_` (current length included);
      - conclusion with `B_u^{1/6} · XLK n_ u` in place of `B_u^{1/6} · STsupXiLK`.
    - The current pin implies the primed one, so the primed pin is weaker.
    - No merged Lean consumes `STOeqNQ`: it appears only in `inst_OeqNQ` and the registry (`Test/Axioms.lean:113`). Its planned consumers S3-18b, S3-24b and S3-26 are unwritten.
    - New file, as in §57 (CLAUDE.md §5.3). Move the owed registry line.
    - Paper-delta: "the random self-term of `(am;asoiuw)` is replaced by a deterministic control of the current length, as in RBM2D `STOeqPT` (required by §7)".
  - **The absorption moves to the proof of `STXiBoot` (S3-18b).** It becomes a finite deterministic bootstrap:
    - start from the crude deterministic bound `Ξ̂_n ≤ N^{C₀}` (`STXiLKM_crudeN`, with `STKbound` for `𝒦`);
    - apply the primed non-alternating and alternating endpoints with `XLK n_ :=` the current control, which gives `Ξ̂_n ≺ B^{1/6}·control + RHS`;
    - `B_u^{1/6} ≤ N^{-c}` for a fixed `c > 0` under `(eq:WO)`, which S3-18b's preflight must check, so `⌈C₀/c⌉ + 1` rounds give `STXiBoot`'s deterministic right side (`Prec` is stable under finitely many rounds).
    - The alternating chain (S3-14…S3-18b, unwritten) has the same self-term in its drift (`ℰ^{(𝓛−𝒦)×(𝓛−𝒦)}`). It must be pinned the same way, with a deterministic current-length level, or it hits Gap 2 at S3-18b.
  - **(R) Route for the primed pin, cheaper than A (no `GoodSetN′`/`GridGoodN′` copy).**
    - Use the merged `GoodSetN` at the crude level `Φ = N^{C₀}`. Then `hX` and `hY` hold deterministically, while `hQ` keeps the real `Λ`. Use only its level-free clauses (Herm, Dec, Va, Vb) and (D4).
    - Intersect with a new set `G_lin`: (D1′), (D2′) and (D3′) at separate deterministic levels (the lower controls, and `Φ₂` as above).
    - Take the exit time from `gridExitTauN`, prove measurability of `G_lin`, and get `G_lin` with high probability from `stSEforLn_holds` as in answer 2. Get `hdrift` from `G_lin`.
    - Budget: `tbInitNonAltN`, `tbQvNonAltN`, and `tbDriftN` at the linear level.
    - Add a primed `subGaussStop` wrapper.
    - Then the grid endpoint, and the flow endpoint, which is uniform over `TimeIcc` (`Prec`) by the continuity net.
    - **Size.** About three tickets: pin′ + `G_lin` + linear budget (≈ 1000–1300 lines); grid endpoint (≈ 1200); flow endpoint (≈ 800–1000). That is the same count as A's S3-11b + S3-12a/b, and it saves A's copy of `GridGoodN` (1249 lines, about 470 of them measurability). The ST-3 plan stays at 41 (§58 already added two).
- **Who decides.**
  - Changing the pin is the only way to close the gap. By §58 (2), a pin change goes to Jun once A fails.
  - For that question: the primed form is RBM2D's form, and §7 (Jun, "A") chose RBM2D's route, so `STNQConcl` is the departure from it.
  - One question; estimated +0 tickets compared with A; about one day.
