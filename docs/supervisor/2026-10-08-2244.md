## Answers

### R1 (Dom route) — (a), in the molecule-provenance form; one design ticket first

**Why the paper is right, and what it actually uses.** The paper's sentence "all internal vertices lie in `D_{>ℓ}`" (`7_8:1633`) / "in `D_{≤ℓ}`" (`7_8:1648`) is about the vertices of the **auxiliary graph**, i.e. molecules, not about vertex labels of the locally standard graph. Two facts make it true:
1. `S^{(B)}` is nearest-neighbour on blocks (`1_2:94`), so in `f` the vertices `β^{(k)}` and `α^{(k)}` lie in one molecule, at block distance ≤ 1.
2. The Gaussian-IBP / `LocStep` expansion never creates a new molecule: every new internal vertex is attached by a waved edge to an existing one, and merges only fuse molecules (this is also why `n_M ≤ p` holds, T2344 (a)(i) row 6).
So every molecule of every output graph contains at least one initial `β^{(k)}`. The `p` indicator weights `w_k(blk ℓ β^{(k)})` therefore restrict **every** auxiliary internal vertex, up to the molecule radius `Rb`. The far argument then needs one long edge per path; it gets it at `α^{(k)}`, which sits on path `𝔓_k`.

**Consequences for the design:**
- **(a) is the right route.** What is missing is exactly the vertex-weighted identity `E[Π_k f^{w_k}] = Σ_r E[Σ_{ℓ_i} Π_k w_k(ℓ(π_r β^{(k)})) term_r]` with a provenance map `π_r : I₀ → I_r` per output. A full twin of the engine is **not** needed if the design proves one generic lemma: each `LocStepX` constructor comes with a label-compatible map from input vertices to output vertices (an inclusion for IBP/T-steps; a quotient for merges/partitions), and its `eval` identity holds pointwise in the labels of the old vertices. The weighted identity then follows by pulling the weight back along `π_r`. The step lemmas are already generic in `E I` (`LWEngine.lean:198-320`); only `LocStepX` and its `eval` (`:391-455`) are fixed at `Fin 2`. **Estimate: one row, 800–1500 lines.** If some constructor's proof is not pointwise in old labels (for example, a sum reindexing that renames old vertices), the cost rises to a real twin, 1500–2500 lines (one large row).
- **Downstream side.** `GtoAG` / the far and near pins should read the weight at the **molecule** level: an aux vertex is in the domain enlarged by `Rb`. The enlargement costs `sT(ℓ - Rb)/sT(ℓ) ≤ e^{½√(Rb/ℓ_t)}`, the same order as the G2 loss and `≺`-valid with the R2 radius. So the far pin `AnpFarAndAt` with "all internal vertices in the (enlarged) domain" can stay as merged in shape. The design must confirm that the merged `farDAnd` / `lwMomExp_nearD` accept a radius parameter, or name the one-line restatement.
- **(b) has no cheaper path.** I checked the two obvious ones. (1) A weight on the black edge breaks the Stein row sums; T2344 is right. (2) Bounding `f^{>ℓ}` as `f - f^{≤ℓ}`, or through the unrestricted expansion, fails because `sT(·∧ℓ)` gives no decay past `ℓ`. The far-label volume `× sT(ℓ)^{2k}` is not small at `ℓ ≈ (log W)^{3/2}ℓ_t` (T2344 `ratio_far`). The restriction is mathematically necessary.
- **(c) Externalising `β`** (option (iii)) is also unsound for a different reason: R-steps expand only at `x : I`, so an external `β` would never be expanded, and the outputs would not be locally standard at `β`.
- **Dedicated design: yes.** Open **one design ticket** (LW-13b-D, not a Fable review). Scope: the provenance lemma statement over `LocStepX`, a constructor-by-constructor table "inclusion / quotient / pointwise?", the molecule-level domain statements for the far and near pins with the `Rb` enlargement, the G4 three-way split, the G2 radius (R2), and a row estimate. ### R2 (G2) — accept, with the radius set by the S-tail, not by `ρ`
`(log W)²` in `LWMoment.lean:1048` is the per-edge truncation radius at which the waved-edge tail `e^{-c r/2}` beats every power of `N` (`LWMoment.lean:471-476`, `:924`). It is not forced by `LWGbyXi`, whose `R` is free subject to `2R+1 ≤ ρ` (`AuxGraph2.lean:1114`). Any `r = (log W)^{1+δ}` also gives a super-polynomial tail. With `δ = ½`:
- the shift loss is `e^{½√(2ρ/ℓ_t)} ≤ e^{C(log W)^{3/4}}`, which is `≺`-negligible (asymptotic only, like row 1 of the T2344 table; acceptable because `≺` is asymptotic);
- the paper's own thresholds use `(log W)^{3/2}` (`7_8:95-97, 1602`), so the regime split and the radius agree.

So: an exp-class twin of `lwXiClaim_holds` that does not go through `LWPsiAll`, using the shift monotonicity of `sT` directly (`√r - √(r-ρ) ≤ √ρ`), at `Rb = K(log W)^{3/2}` with a `(log W)^{3/2}` copy of the tail lemma. The paper does not derive this step separately. It is absorbed into its `≺` on `ξ` (`7_8:1653`) under the hypothesis `|G| ≺ sT(|a-b|∧ℓ)` (`7_8:26`). Record it as paper-delta T2344c.
