## A. REQ-2026-10-09-2010: opening BA stage K

### A.0 Independent check of F1 (route-relevant, so re-derived here)

- **Paper.** `(eq:KMloop)` is `𝓜^{(k)}_{σ,a} = tr ∏_{i=1}^k (M(σ_i) E_{a_i})` (`1_2:1003`). The trace expands as follows:
  - (A_1 E_{a_1} A_2 E_{a_2} ⋯ A_k E_{a_k})_{xx} links `A_i` to the pair `(a_{i-1}, a_i)`;
  - with `M = M^{(B)} ⊗ I_{W^d}` and the normalisation `W^{-(k-1)d}` of the band remark, this gives `W^{-(k-1)d} ∏ M(σ_i)_{a_{i-1}a_i}`.
  - The tree rule `A:571` assigns the same: the edge `b_{j-1} → b_j` carries `σ_j` and `a_j = b_j`.
- **Lean, the other merged conventions.** The merged G-loop `loopM` is `trace (List.ofFn fun i => Gres H z (σ i) * Eblk (a i)).prod` (`GLoopFlow.lean:91-92`), so `G(σ_i)` also sits between `a_{i-1}` and `a_i`. The cuts `cutGlueL/R` (`TreeRep.lean:75-82`) follow the same convention.
- **Lean, `BAMLoop`.** The merged `BAMLoop` (`FlowPins.lean:274-276`) zips `σ` with `a.zip (a.rotate 1)`, that is `M(σ_i)_{a_i a_{i+1}}`. That is the paper's value at the charge vector shifted by one place. For `n ≤ 2` it agrees, because `M(±)` are symmetric. For `n ≥ 3` with mixed charges it does not: probe witness 14 against 15, and the audit's own check gives 0.11 against a size of 0.09.
- **Consequence.** K-loops and G-loops of the BA carrier use different conventions at `n ≥ 3`. So the owed initial-data pin `STLKgL (baFMz sz0 zSeq) … (fun _ => 0)` (`BA/UNPins.lean:1016`; registry `Test/Axioms.lean:139`) and every owed `STKboundgL (baFMz …)` premise are statements about the wrong carrier.
- **Grep.** `BAMLoop` occurs only at `FlowPins.lean:274, 283, 286`. The other `.rotate` and `.zip` uses in `BA/`, `Loop/` and `Universality/` are the rotation helpers of `KLWard`/`KLUnique` and `σ.zip a` words; none is another pairing of this kind. `BAKloop` is unfolded only in `baFM_eqAt` (`Step1Fam.lean:270`), which is a congruence in `(lam0 n, E n)` and does not depend on the body.
- **History.** The defect came in with BA-C1a (T2197, merged b750bf3, 2026-10-06 00:22 UTC) and was visible from then on. No ticket between then and T2360 proved anything about `BAMLoop` at `n ≥ 3`, so nothing has to be redone.
