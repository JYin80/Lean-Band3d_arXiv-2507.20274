## 4. Q4 — LW-02 and the `m`-uniform expansion

### 4.1 The block is real
- `lw_localregular (p) (m) (c) …` is `lvl1_lemma_size` applied at `m` (`LocalRegular6d.lean:1107`; `LWLvl1.lean:4047`). Its lists come from `lvl1_exists_step : ∃ outs, LocStep m P outs` (`:3655`) inside a well-founded recursion. For different `m` they are unrelated witnesses.
- In `LWMoment` (`LWPins.lean:325`), `∃ c` precedes `z`, and `m_n = mE(STflowE z n)` moves with `n`.
- Even inside one `Prec` (fixed `z`), the bound at `n` uses the list at `m_n`. The constants of `lwAnp_holds` are per graph (`∃ c`, `∀ᶠ n` per `Γ`), so a list that changes with `n` has no uniform threshold. This is T2297 F1 (a)–(c), confirmed.

### 4.2 No cheaper route
- **Compactness of `{|m| = 1}` gives nothing.** The merged statement relates `outs(m)` and `outs(m′)` in no way, so there is no continuity to use.
- **Finiteness of graph types gives nothing either.** The merged conclusions bound `ord`, `nM`, `nV − nW` and `Normal` (`LocalRegular6d.lean:1109-1114`). They do not bound `nV`, `nW` separately, `|outs|`, or `‖coeff‖`. So there is no finite class to take a maximum over.
  - One cheap fact is true: `‖coeff‖ = |m|^{j+j′} = 1` on `|m| = 1` (T2297 F1; T2288 output 4). It is not in the statement either.
- **Every repair re-opens the recursion of `lvl1_lemma`.** There are two ways:
  - (i) add uniform bounds (`|outs|`, `nS`, `nV`, `nW`, `‖coeff‖`), then prove that the per-`Γ` constants of `lwAnp_holds` are uniform over a finite iso-class;
  - (ii) produce one `m`-free list.
  - (ii) is less work: it needs no iso-class argument, and with one fixed finite list the constants are maxima over that list.
